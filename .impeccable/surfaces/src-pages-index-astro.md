# Surface brief: example.com home (marketing)

Scope: the single long-form marketing page at `/`. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience & job: owner/operator of a growing Indian SMB deciding whether FlowX365
can replace spreadsheets + WhatsApp + separate billing. Action: request a demo
through the on-site form, with sales@example.com as the fallback. The site does
not present a Sign in action.
Proof on hand: the real product only (recreated UI with labelled illustrative
data). No customers, stats, testimonials, pricing (that lives only on /pricing; see PRICING.md), or unshipped modules. AI
enablement and payroll support remain pending product-owner confirmation; do not
add, remove, or strengthen either marketing claim until they are confirmed.

Memorable moment: one quotation's lines impressed, unchanged, through Sales
Order, Delivery Note and Tax Invoice while the sheet colour changes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Write it once; the impression passes through every copy beneath. The
page is a bill book: cover, numbered sheets, copies. It refuses the category
default of a dashboard-in-a-laptop hero followed by a module icon grid.

OWN-WORLD: Navy book-cover fields (#0F1520/#1A2340); sheets as full-bleed
flat fields in statutory copy colours: Original white #F7F8FA, Duplicate pink,
Triplicate canary yellow. Carbon-paper blue-violet is the colour of every
"impressed" line (content carried forward). Orange #F5820A only for the primary
action and the live state. Perforated sheet edges, printed serial numbers
(No. 01…), form-field rules. Archivo (wide caps for form headers and display),
one mono for serials. No gradients, glass, glow, or rounded card grids.

STORY: The visitor sees that this is a business system, encounters a concise
security trust sheet covering dedicated customer databases, encryption and
data isolation, then feels the pain of one order typed five times. They watch
one entry travel through every document, see the real screens (CRM, trackers,
stock, print, the team's day), get answers to the questions an owner actually
asks, then request a demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy cover. “Write it once. Let it flow.” stays in four centred
display lines, flanked by the actual document chain: Quotation over Sales Order
on the left, Tax Invoice over Delivery Note on the right. The supporting line
reads “One connected platform for your entire business.” A bottom-centred
“Scroll to see how it flows” cue links to No. 01; Request a demo remains in the
navigation and there is no separate cover CTA or brand lockup. The documents
enter from the edges, lean away from the pointer on springs and open outward
with the scroll lift. At 900px and below they fan up from the bottom instead.
The unnumbered Security sheet remains physically next, followed by No. 01,
What it is. Decorative slogan copies are hidden at rest and from assistive
technology; reduced motion disables their ripple, trail and lift movement.

FORM: Triplicate Bill Book (Impeccable's pick, rank 1 on the grounded list;
the roll assigned rank 5, Dispatch Manifest). Seed key 94124120.
Signature interaction: the pinned chain scene, where each sheet lifts to reveal
the next copy beneath, earlier copies stack ghosted, the lead id and line items
never change, and stock ticks down at dispatch.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
